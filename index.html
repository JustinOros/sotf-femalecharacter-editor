Character

Body

Skin tone darkens or tints the skin. White keeps the original.

Outfit pieces

Each woman has her own outfits. Switch the Woman above and you see and change only her set. Fit moves the clothing out from the body (looser) or in toward it (tighter). Layer sets which piece goes on top where two pieces overlap: higher goes over lower, for example boots 40 over pants 20 tucks the pants into the boots. Where the lower piece pokes through, it is pulled just inside the higher one. The eye button hides a piece while you look underneath. It is not saved or exported.

Save

Your changes are kept in this browser while you work. Export the zip and email it back when you are done. The zip holds all 8 women and each one's own outfits.
